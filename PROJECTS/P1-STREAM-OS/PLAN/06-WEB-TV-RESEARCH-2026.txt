PART 1 — DUNIYA ABHI KAHAN HAI (2026 FAST RESEARCH)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  FAST = Free Ad-Supported Streaming TV
  MATLAB: bilkul TV jaisi feel (channel chalta rahe, tune karo),
  par paisa NAHI — beech mein ads. Gharelu cable ka free digital bhai.

  NUMBERS (research se, 2026):
    - Samsung TV Plus : 100 MILLION+ monthly users,
                        4,300 channels, 30+ deshon mein
                        (har Samsung TV mein pehle se installed!)
    - Tubi (Fox)      : 80+ MILLION monthly users,
                        260 live channels + 50,000 movies/shows
    - Pluto TV        : ~80 MILLION users, 250+ channels, 30+ desh
    - The Roku Channel: 500+ channels
    - FAST = US ki TOTAL TV watching ka ~4%+ (Hulu/Prime se zyada!)
    - Growth: ~15% har saal (Goldman Sachs projection tak 2027)

  SAB SMART-TV COMPANIES ab apna FAST service banati hain:
    Samsung, LG, Vizio, TCL, Hisense — sab.

  TRANSLATION TERE LIYE:
    - "Subscription thakela" duniya bhar mein hai. Log FREE
      legal TV ki taraf bhaag rahe hain.
    - India mein bhi yahi wave: JioCinema/Hotstar ka free
      with-ads plan, MX Player ka free model, DD Free Dish
      (desh ka free-to-air DTH — isi soch ka purana desi bhai).
    - AUR: koi bhi bada player INDIA-KE-LIYE, BANGLA-FIRST,
      browser-only, zero-install TV nahi bana raha.
      YE GAP HAI. Tera mauka. 🎯

PART 4 — 5 HARD PARTS (AUR UNKA ILAAJ) ⚠️
----------------------------------------------------------------

  HARD 1 — CORS (SABSE BADA DUSHMAN) 🔥
    SACH: Browser mein khelne ke liye stream-server ko
    Access-Control-Allow-Origin header bhejna padta hai.
    VLC chala leta hai, BROWSER nahi — kyunki VLC pe
    CORS nahi lagta, browser pe lagta hai.
    AUR: sirf .m3u8 nahi — segments (.ts), keys, subtitles
    SAB pe alag alag CORS check hota hai!
    ILAAJ (3 raste, research-verified):
      a) Jo streams open-allow karte hain -> seedha chalao
         (bahut saare public streams allow karte hain)
      b) Baaki ke liye -> apna chhota PROXY (Cloudflare
         Worker jaisa) jo manifest+segments relay kare
         (Archive/Book 3-7 mein banayenge — backend kaam)
      c) Jo bilkul na chale -> honestly "ye browser mein
         nahi chalega" dikhao (Wisp bhi yahi karta hai)

  HARD 2 — DEAD LINKS (80-90% links mare hote hain!) 💀
    Isi liye duniya bhar ke playlists bekaar lagti hain.
    ILAAJ = TERA APNAA P4 m3u-health-check!
      Playlist mein daalne se PEHLE har link ping karo.
      Zinda ho tabhi dikhao. Yehi tera secret sauce hai —
      jiske paas ye engine hai, usi ki list sach mein chalti hai.

  HARD 3 — GEO-BLOCK (1,210 streams India se nahi chalte)
    ILAAJ: pehle se filter + metadata mein mark karke
    user ko pehle hi bata do. Jhoota "play" button kabhi nahi.

  HARD 4 — NOT-24/7 (1,803 links kabhi kabhi)
    ILAAJ: health-check roz chalao + "abhi OFF aaya" label.

  HARD 5 — http-on-https (mixed content block)
    ILAAJ: https site pe http stream block hota hai —
    proxy (HARD 1b) ye bhi solve karta hai.

PART 5 — LEGAL LINE (saaf-saaf, kabhi mat bhoolna) ⚖️
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. iptv-org ek INDEX hai public streams ka — HOST nahi.
     Wo sirf batata hai "ye link public web pe milta hai."
  2. blocklist.json ZAROORI hai — DMCA/NSFW channels usme
     hote hain, unhe KABHI mat dikhaana. (Filters mein pehla rule!)
  3. Tera use-case (apna web app, khud khushi, learning,
     portfolio) = THEEK hai — kyunki tu link hi dikha raha,
     content host nahi kar raha.
  4. Kabhi isme PAISA mat lena bina legal salah ke.
  5. Paid subscription content kabhi isme mat ghusaana.
  Ye 5 niyam tode to project CV mein achha nahi, KHATRE mein
  hoga. Follow karo to ye tera sabse saaf legal masterpiece hai.
